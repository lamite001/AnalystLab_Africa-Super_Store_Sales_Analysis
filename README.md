# AnalystLab_Africa-Super_Store_Sales_Analysis
# Superstore Sales Executive Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI executive dashboard built on the Superstore
Sales dataset, created as part of my Junior BI Analyst assignment with
AnalystLab Africa Consulting.

## Business Problem
Leadership needs a clear view of sales performance, profitability and 
the drivers behind them, so they can decide where to invest, where to
cut discounts and which segments to prioritise.

## Dataset
- Source: Superstore Sales dataset (Kaggle)
- 9,994 rows, 21 columns
- Covers orders, customers, products, regions, sales, discount, quantity and profit

## Tools & Skills
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)
- DAX (measures and KPIs)
- Data modelling and visualisation






## Dashboard Features
- **KPI cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, Average Discount
- **Visuals:** sales and profit trend over time, sales by region, category and
-  sub-category performance, top and bottom products, customer segment analysis, discount vs profit
- **Slicers:** Year, Region, Category, Segment
- **Executive summary page** with key insights

## Key Insights
1. [Insight 1 - Technology drives the highest profit margin at 0.17%]
2. [Insight 2 - High discounts above 20% are linked to negative profit]
3. [Insight 3 - West region generates the most sales but has the lowest margin]
4. [Insight  4 - Sub-category Bookcases is consistently loss-making]

## Recommendations
1. Review and cap discounting on loss-making sub-categories
2. Focus marketing on the highest-margin segments
3. Investigate underperforming regions
4. Promote high-margin products
5. Monitor margin monthly with this dashboard
